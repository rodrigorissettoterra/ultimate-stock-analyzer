# Investment Research Disclaimer

Ultimate Stock Analyzer is educational and research software. Its outputs are generated from data, configurable models and automated text analysis. They may be incomplete, delayed, incorrect or unsuitable for a specific investor. Scores are not guarantees of future performance and do not replace professional financial, accounting, legal or investment advice.

The system intentionally uses classifications such as `VERY_ATTRACTIVE`, `ATTRACTIVE`, `WATCH`, `WAIT`, `AVOID` and `BLOCKED` rather than executing trades. Users remain responsible for verifying source data and their own decisions.
